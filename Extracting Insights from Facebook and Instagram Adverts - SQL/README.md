## Overview
This project analyzed key performance indicators for Facebook and Instagram advertising campaigns, including ad spend, reach, engagement, likes, followers, and month-on-month growth. The goal was to explain what was driving results and recommend how to optimize future spend. The work followed a structured flow of data extraction, cleaning, and exploratory analysis in SQL, with results presented in an interactive Power BI dashboard.

## Key findings
- Ad spend jumped **198.74% from March to April**, a spike worth understanding before repeating it.
- Engagement outcomes (Facebook likes and Instagram followers) varied month to month, pointing to which targeting and creative choices resonated.
- Performance patterns across the channels showed where budget was and was not delivering return.

## Recommendations
Track ad performance continuously and reallocate budget toward the highest ROI, use each platform's own analytics on impressions and click-through, study what drove the strong January engagement and the April spend surge, and adjust to market trends as they shift.

## Skills demonstrated
Data extraction and cleaning, exploratory data analysis, SQL for analysis, Power BI dashboard design, and team leadership.
