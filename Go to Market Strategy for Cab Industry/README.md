# Go-to-Market Strategy for Cab Industry Investment

> A go-to-market analysis comparing two US cab companies to guide an investment decision. The recommendation: **invest in Yellow Cab**, which delivered **$44M in profit vs Pink Cab's $5M** and nearly triple the profit per trip.

**Tools:** `Power BI` · `Excel`
**Type:** Investment and market analysis

## Objective
Provide actionable insights to help an investment firm choose the more profitable of two cab companies, Pink Cab versus Yellow Cab.

## Key findings
- **Profit:** Yellow Cab $44M vs Pink Cab $5M.
- **Profit per trip:** Yellow Cab $160.26 vs Pink Cab $62.65, nearly triple.
- **Top cities by users:** New York City (302K), San Francisco (214K), Chicago (164K).
- **Payment mix:** 60% of payments made by card.
- **Demand timing:** trips peak on Fridays, Saturdays, and Sundays, and quarterly volume and profit peak in Q4.
- **Customer profile:** most users earn $5K to $25K a year and are aged 18 to 40, with similar gender splits across both companies.

## Recommendations
Invest in Yellow Cab given its far stronger economics, expand in the largest markets (New York, San Francisco, Chicago, Los Angeles, and Washington), deploy extra cabs on weekends to capture peak demand, and target marketing at middle-income riders aged 18 to 40.

## Skills demonstrated
Comparative profit analysis, customer segmentation, seasonality and regional analysis, Power BI dashboarding, and translating findings into an investment recommendation.
